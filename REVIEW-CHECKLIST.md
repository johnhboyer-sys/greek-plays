   *Spot check, 2026-09-08 20:20 (orchestrator, Birds, 8 of 101 flags read against the Greek and the 1912 source).* The low-ratio flags (five of eight) are packed antilabe lines — one speech measured against a five-speaker block — and harmless. Options for John: accept as is; a narrow re-sync rule for same-speaker runs where the English count is short (fixes (a)); line-scoped English overrides in the manifest for (b)/(c).
