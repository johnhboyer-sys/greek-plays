## Gaps and checks

Most thematic comedy leads have no proven link to a staging. Many files need a visual check of the figure outline, nudity, and painted figure height. Null figure heights mean the 800 px rule has not been confirmed. Where the exact file licence text was not seen, the JSON sets licence_verified to false. Empty file_url fields mean the largest direct image URL was not captured. The Agamemnon fragment may fail the whole-figure rule, and the Ajax image reveals the ending. Some ArchaiOptix file pages warn that the museums may limit photography even though the photographer has marked the exact Commons file CC BY-SA; review that conflict before use.
