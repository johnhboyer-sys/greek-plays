Reading view: three sketches

Two real passages in each. Agamemnon 1202–1213 is a fast exchange of single lines. Medea 1271–1292 has the children calling from inside the house, a printed transposition, and changes of voice inside the Chorus. The Greek is the open edition each translation is paired with (Smyth's Loeb; Murray's Oxford text). Speaker colours are the reader's own. Greek and English are set at the same size throughout.

The reading view stays neutral: no author tint. Stage directions in the English are the translator's, and say so. Line numbers show every fifth line, the first line, and any line printed out of order.
