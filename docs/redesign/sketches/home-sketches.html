Eight rough home pages

Each sketch tries a different idea, using the real names, Greek titles, play counts and production notes from the repo. They are rough on purpose. Look for the idea you like, not the finish.

The drawings are stand-ins: four figures cropped from the 1841 engraving of the Pronomos vase (Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0), which shows a satyr-play cast. They test size and placement only. Final drawings would show each playwright's own plays. The play links under each name are my picks.

1 Frieze

All four playwrights stand on one ground line, like figures on a vase, with their names under them.

React to: Does one shared picture make the four feel like one theatre? Is a figure 330px tall big enough?

3 Red-figure ground

A black ground with the drawings and names in clay colour, the way red-figure vases leave figures in clay against black gloss. Only the home and author pages would look like this.

React to: Too dark, or does it give the site a face? Would you want the reader itself to stay light?

6 Timeline

The four lives on one line of years, with each dated play marked where it was staged. Plays without a firm date are listed, not placed. Filled marks are recorded dates; open marks are "c." dates.

React to: Is it useful to see that Sophocles and Euripides died the same year and Frogs came the next? Or is this a page for later, not the home?
