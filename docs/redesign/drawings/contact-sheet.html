Drawing test: five drawings, revised

Each drawing is fitted into a 400px and a 120px square slot, in light and dark, on the warm paper and on each author tint. The line under each slot is the ground line. The dark grounds are provisional: DESIGN.md says the dark tints are still to be made.

The drawings are filled shapes traced by potrace, with fill currentColor, and one line weight: 0.225% of the drawing's longer side, so drawings in the same slot show the same line. In the 120px slots the page adds a 0.5px stroke in currentColor so the lines stay visible; the 400px slots have none.

Dark mode: the lines stay dark and the figure is filled with clay (#c4b294), as on a red-figure vase, rather than turning the lines light. Each SVG carries the figure's area as a second path, filled with var(--drawing-body, none): transparent unless the page sets it.
